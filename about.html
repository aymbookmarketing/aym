My Story

How I Became a Book Marketer

Eight years ago, I met an author at a local event right here in Texas. She had just self-published her first novel after three years of writing, editing, and revising. She was proud, excited, and full of hope. Her eyes lit up when she talked about her characters like they were real people.

But when I asked her about her book marketing plan, her face dropped.

She did not have one.

She thought publishing the book was the finish line. She thought readers would simply find her book on Amazon and start buying it. She had no social media strategy, no email list, no promotional plan, and no idea how book marketing actually worked.

Six months later, she told me she had sold fewer than 50 copies.

Her book was incredible. I read it. The writing was beautiful, the story was gripping, and the characters stayed with me long after I finished the last page. But nobody knew it existed.

That moment changed everything for me.

I realized that the publishing world was full of talented authors who were being failed by a system that teaches you how to write a book but never teaches you how to market one. Authors were spending years perfecting their manuscripts and then releasing them into the world with zero book marketing support.

I decided right then that I would dedicate my career to solving that problem.

I started studying book marketing obsessively. I learned Amazon algorithms, social media strategies, email marketing, paid advertising, content marketing, author branding, and launch planning. I tested everything. I tracked what worked and what did not. I worked with my first few authors for free just to prove that my book marketing strategies could deliver real results.

And they did.

Fast forward 8 years, and A.Y.M Book Marketing has helped 57 authors market over 300 books across every genre imaginable. From first-time authors publishing their debut novels to established writers looking to revive their backlist, I have seen it all and I have built book marketing strategies for all of it.

Today, A.Y.M Book Marketing is a full-service book marketing agency based in Texas, and I work with authors from all over the world. My approach has not changed since that first conversation with that author at the event. I still believe that every great book deserves a great book marketing strategy. And I still take every author's success personally.

Your book is not just a product to me. It is your voice, your story, your legacy. And I will market it with the same passion you put into writing it.
