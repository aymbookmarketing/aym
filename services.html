Your author name is your most valuable asset. Long after individual book campaigns end, your author brand is what keeps readers coming back.

I help you build a cohesive, professional author brand across all platforms. This includes your author bio, your visual identity, your tone of voice, your social media presence, and the overall impression readers get when they discover your name online.

I handle your social media book marketing across all major platforms including Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Twitter/X, YouTube, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Threads, and Goodreads.
